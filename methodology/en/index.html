Analysis methodology

How we turn motion into scores.

Scores in Pose Coach are a directional reference — a higher score means your movement pattern is closer to the published reference profile for that technique. They are not a verdict, and they are not a medical or clinical assessment.

Pose Coach scoring

Badminton

Badminton swing scoring

Five dimensions — completeness, tempo, joint angles, stability, kinetic chain (beta) — scored 0–100 with an S/A/B/C/D grade, plus the three things to work on next.

Running form scoring

Four technical dimensions — symmetry, trunk lean, hip extension, arm swing — scored 0–100. A directional read of form, separate from your speed or race time.

Running efficiency scoring

Cadence, vertical ratio, time symmetry and stride efficiency — weighted 0–100, with optional Running Power. How economically you run, separate from speed.

How the measurements are taken

Pose Coach uses Apple ARKit body-tracking to estimate 3D joint positions from the device camera at up to 60 frames per second. From this data we compute joint angles, movement timing, and technique patterns, then compare them against reference values derived from aggregated motion samples and published sport-technique standards.

These measurements are estimates from a single phone camera. They are not laboratory-grade and accuracy depends on lighting, framing, distance, and how completely your body stays in frame. Tips are generated from the same comparison and highlight the dimensions with the largest gap from the reference.

Where the reference standards come from

Running speed is projected from peak pace measured over a short interval, not a full timed event.

Not medical advice — check with a doctor

The analysis, scores, and recommendations in Pose Coach are for sports training and educational purposes only. They are not a substitute for:

  • Professional sports coaching
  • Physiotherapy or sports-medicine assessment
  • Medical diagnosis or treatment

Always check with a qualified doctor or healthcare professional before starting a new training program, and before making any decisions about your health. If you experience pain, discomfort, dizziness, or are recovering from an injury, stop and consult a healthcare professional before continuing.

Scores and tips do not diagnose injuries, health conditions, or physical limitations, and they are not a substitute for professional advice.
